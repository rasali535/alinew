# Mari AI — Proactive Intelligence & Briefing Engine

## 1. 5 Core Briefing Questions
Mari Proactive Intelligence automatically computes:
1. **What Changed?**: Delta analysis on reach, follower count, revenue additions, and task completions.
2. **Why Does It Matter?**: Strategic impact relative to regional benchmarks and historical trends.
3. **What Needs Attention?**: SLA flags, aging proposal follow-ups, and urgent operational items.
4. **What Opportunities Exist?**: High-performing formats (e.g. video outperforming static posts by 2.3×).
5. **What Should Happen Next?**: 1-click suggested actions linking to Growth Studio, CRM, and Task manager.

## 2. Structured Insights Model
Insight types generated:
- `OPPORTUNITY`: Commercial & reach growth avenues.
- `RISK`: Overdue tasks, aging deals, or declining metrics.
- `TREND`: Macro regional market shifts.
- `RECOMMENDATION`: Tactical execution advice.
- `PERFORMANCE_INSIGHT`: Metric comparisons.
