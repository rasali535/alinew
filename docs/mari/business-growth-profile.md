# Mari AI — Business Growth Profile

## 1. Concept & Purpose
The **Business Growth Profile** represents Mari's living strategic understanding of the customer's organization.
It unifies:
- **Commercial Foundation**: Revenue model, target market, ideal customer profile (ICP).
- **Live Performance State**: Active pipeline value, customer counts, marketing channel reach.
- **Explainable Growth Score**: Calculated score with positive drivers and negative risks.
- **Prioritized Opportunities**: Impact, urgency, effort, and confidence-scored actions.
- **Growth Memory History**: Real recorded outcomes and lessons learned.

---

## 2. Zero-Hallucination Policy
- Metrics are pulled directly from verified database ledgers and OAuth Graph APIs.
- Every insight carries explicit Data Provenance: `VERIFIED`, `USER_PROVIDED`, `INFERRED`, `AI_RECOMMENDATION`.
- If data is unavailable or insufficient, Mari explicitly reports: *"Insufficient telemetry for a reliable calculation."*
